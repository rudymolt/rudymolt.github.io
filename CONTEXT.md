## Terms

### Ideas portal

**Definition.** The public homepage that indexes Rudy's software and published
explainers at `rudymolt.github.io`.

**Related.** Published path, software card.

**Not the same as.** A published explainer, which is one destination linked from
the portal.

### Operational playbook

**Definition.** The V0.4 agent-engineering process installed into this repository
from `rudymolt/ai-engineering-playbook`.

**Related.** Published playbook.

**Not the same as.** The published playbook, which is visitor-facing content.

### Paper design

**Definition.** The approved desktop and mobile artboards in the Paper.design
file “Rudy Molt — Ideas Portal Redesign”.

**Related.** Ideas portal.

### Published path

**Definition.** A visitor-facing explainer or visualisation linked from the Ideas
index.

**Related.** Ideas portal, published playbook.

### Published playbook

**Definition.** The human-facing AI Engineering Playbook V0.4 pages under
`agent-engineering-playbook/`.

**Related.** Operational playbook, published path.

### Software card

**Definition.** A portal entry for a Rudy Molt software project such as RuOps,
RuChamps, or RuComps.

**Related.** Ideas portal.
