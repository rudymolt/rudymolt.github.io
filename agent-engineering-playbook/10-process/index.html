The 13 Stages, In 4 Phases

Five stages are the everyday five — the ones you will actually drive. The rest are tagged agent mostly handles this. Every stage shows what it looks like on the booking-form feature.

What V0.4 changes. The stage map is still the same. V0.4 adds an optional delivery mission around stages 07–10: a human approves the exact scope and limits, the builder works inside that envelope, a fresh checker independently verifies the result, and the normal route stops at an exact pull request. K4.1 fresh-agent merge is a separate, off-by-default project choice; it does not include deploy or release.
